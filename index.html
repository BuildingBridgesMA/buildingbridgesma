Who we are

Two generations, one community

Building Bridges is a youth-led organization from Southborough and Northborough, Massachusetts. We connect local students with seniors so they can talk, play, learn from each other and give back to the towns they share.

Teens and seniors live on the same streets and shop at the same stores, but outside of family they rarely get to spend real time together. We're here to change that, one visit, one event and one conversation at a time.

About us

Bringing generations together, close to home

Who we are, what we believe and where we're headed.

Our mission

Building Bridges connects young people and seniors in Southborough and Northborough through friendship, shared events and community service, so every generation in our towns feels seen, valued and connected.

Our vision

Two towns where every generation knows each other by name

We picture a community where a high schooler and a grandparent down the street can swap stories, trade skills and show up for each other.

Our story

Building Bridges was started by students from Southborough and Northborough who saw a gap in our community. Seniors have decades of stories, skills and wisdom to share. Students have energy, fresh ideas and plenty to learn. Outside of family, though, the two groups rarely cross paths.

So we decided to build a bridge between them. We organize events, lend a hand where it's needed and make room for real conversations between generations, right here in the towns we grew up in.

We're just getting started, and we'd love for you to be part of it.

What we believe

Our values

Connection

Real relationships that last longer than one afternoon. We come back, we remember names and we follow through.

Respect

Every person has a story worth hearing. We listen first and treat every neighbor with dignity.

Service

We show up for our community, whether that's running an event, helping with technology or lending a hand around town.

Fun

Bingo, games, good food and good company. Bringing people together should be a good time, and it is.

Our logo

Two people, one bridge

Look closely at our logo. It's two people standing on either side of a bridge: a taller figure in navy and a smaller one in gold, one older and one younger. Their arms reach toward each other and meet in the middle to form the arch.

That's the whole idea behind Building Bridges. A bridge only works when both sides reach out.

Navy stands for the steadiness that comes with experience. Gold stands for the energy young people bring.

About us

          
What is Building Bridges? 
Building Bridges is a student-run organization that connects young people and seniors in Southborough and Northborough, Massachusetts. We bring the two generations together through events, conversation, tech help and community service.

          
Who runs it? 
A team of students from Southborough and Northborough. You can meet everyone on our Team page.

          
Where are you located? 
We serve Southborough and Northborough, two neighboring towns in Worcester County, Massachusetts, between Worcester and Boston. Most of our activities happen at senior centers and other community spaces. See our location page for details.

          
Are you an official nonprofit? 
We're in the process of becoming one! Right now we're a student-run community group working on registering Building Bridges as an official nonprofit. We'll share an update here when it's done.

        

        

          
Volunteering

          
Who can volunteer? 
Students from Southborough, Northborough and nearby who want to spend time with seniors and help out in their community. If you're not sure you'd be a fit, reach out anyway.

          
Do I need any experience? 
Nope. Bring a good attitude and be ready to listen. We'll help with the rest.

          
How much time does it take? 
As much as you can give. You can help out at a single event or get involved in planning. Let us know what works for your schedule.

          
How do I sign up? 
Fill out the volunteer sign-up form and we'll get in touch.

        

        

          
Seniors & families

          
How can I take part as a senior? 
Come to one of our events, or reach out if you'd like help with technology or just some good conversation. You can also ask about us at your local senior center.

          
Can I get help with my phone or computer? 
Yes! Contact us and we'll work out a time and place, usually at a senior center or another community space.

          
Can I sign up a parent or grandparent? 
Of course. Get in touch and tell us a little about them, and we'll help find a good fit.

          
Where do activities take place? 
Most of our activities happen at senior centers and other public community spaces in Southborough and Northborough.

        

        

          
Donations & partners

          
How will my donation be used? 
Donations pay for events and activities, food, program supplies, printing and the costs of becoming an official nonprofit. See our Donate page for more.

          
Is my donation tax-deductible? 
Not yet. We're still in the process of becoming an official nonprofit, so donations aren't tax-deductible right now. We'll update this page when that changes.

          
Can I donate supplies instead of money? 
Absolutely. Check our wish list for the things we use most.

          
How can my business partner with you? 
Sponsor an event, donate food or prizes, host us or help spread the word. Visit our Partners page to learn more.
